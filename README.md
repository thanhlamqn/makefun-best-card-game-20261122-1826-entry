Ember Pact — a complete card duel against the Cinder Witch. Both sides start with 30 life (the witch, boss of the pact, holds 33); mana grows every turn, you play cards from your hand, and the first side to 0 loses. Sixteen original cards across four suits — Flame, Tide, Leaf and Stone — with attack, shield, heal and draw effects, a greedy witch AI, and title, table, victory and defeat screens. Keyboard and mouse: click a glowing card or press 1–7 to play it, Space or Enter to end your turn, R to play again.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No network requests, no libraries: plain static files under game/.
